If songs.db contains the top 100 songs of one listener from 2018, how would you characterize their audio aura?

I think one possible option is to calculate the average energy, danceability and valence and if the average of those 3 values is close to 1,
we could say that the aura is positive and lively, however if it is close to 0 it would be a more sad or melancolic aura.

SELECT (avg(energy) +  avg(danceability) + avg(valence))/ 3 AS aura_score FROM songs;
